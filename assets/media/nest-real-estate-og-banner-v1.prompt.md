# Nest real-estate banner

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. The share-ready WebP is
1200 × 630 pixels. It is used as both the landing-page OG image and persona banner.
The original generated PNG is retained beside it.

## Generation brief

Premium horizontal real-estate brand banner matching Cole and Mirav's warm ivory,
elegant serif, architectural still-life family. Use Nest forest green (#173e31),
ivory (#f3efe5 / #fffaf0), and restrained terracotta (#bd623f). Compose a key badge
and NEST wordmark on the left, with miniature contemporary and gabled homes on
travertine platforms on the right. Olive trees, warm windows, subtle bronze orbit
and sage/terracotta spheres; soft studio daylight and generous margins.

Exact descriptive copy: “YOUR REAL ESTATE PERSONA”, “The right leads.”,
“A clearer next move.”, “Public sources. Private decisions.”,
and “BUYERS · SELLERS · REAL ESTATE”.

No people, character, mascot, CTA message, CTA button, action prompt, UI screenshot,
arrow, watermark, or additional text. The banner does not change landing copy,
chat behaviour, lead discovery or matchmaking policies.

## Hosting

The share image is served from the existing public Nest landing-page repository.
Both environments use its versioned WebP URL, which works without authentication
for visitors and OG crawlers. The generated-media library retains a separate
author-owned upload. The WebP is checked in to the persona and landing-page child.
